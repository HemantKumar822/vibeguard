Open a milestone to see its stories. Each story appears once in the main Issues list and belongs to one milestone. Close stories after their acceptance and verification pass; close a milestone when its stories and delivery outcome are complete.

All stories are open without native blockers. Dependencies in descriptions are guidance for coordinating parallel work. Agree shared contracts and ownership, and satisfy integration prerequisites before closing work. Story 1.1 remains the recommended foundation. No due dates or person assignments are invented.
